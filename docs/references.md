## Evidence Citation Rules

- Record title, author/organization, publication date, retrieval date, and URL for external references.
- Record supplier, part number, quoted price, date, and source link for BOM items.
- Record license and provenance for every image and dataset.
- Link every quantitative presentation claim to its native calculation, test, evaluation, or benchmark file.
- Do not use the reference image in the final submission until its reuse rights are confirmed.
